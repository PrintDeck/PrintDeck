FROM KNOMI TO PRINTDECK

A simple browser
installation.

The USB driver comes from KNOMI’s WCH USB-to-serial hardware—not PrintDeck. We made everything after that a clear, guided process in your desktop browser.

  1. 01

    Connect with USB-C

    Use a USB-C data cable and connect KNOMI directly to your computer. Avoid a USB hub for the first installation.

  2. 02

    Let your system see it

    Windows usually installs the WCH driver automatically through Windows Update.

    Linux works immediately on most distributions; only serial-port permissions may need attention.

    macOS may need the official WCH driver and one approval in Privacy & Security before Chrome can see KNOMI.

  3. 03

    Install PrintDeck

    Open the installer in desktop Chrome or Edge. Select KNOMI, choose First installation / Factory reset, confirm the full erase and wait for 100%. The display restarts with its new PrintDeck experience.

KNOMI QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Know what changes.
Keep your printer untouched.

Straight answers about installing PrintDeck, customizing reactions and using the printer data you already have.

Do I need to edit printer.cfg or install KNOMI-specific macros?

No. PrintDeck is designed to read the standard status data your printer already exposes through Moonraker. Live print status, progress, temperatures, layers and timing do not require PrintDeck-specific macros or changes to G28, bed-mesh or other printer macros. Highly specific reactions appear only when your printer reports enough reliable information to identify them.

Can I add my own GIFs without PlatformIO or rebuilding the firmware?

Yes. Open Web Config, choose an available reaction slot, upload your GIF and preview it before applying it. You can customize slots independently or restore the built-in animation at any time—no C arrays, VS Code project or firmware rebuild required.

Does PrintDeck show useful print information, not just animations?

Yes. Swipe from the reaction screen to see print progress, remaining time, layers, live and target temperatures, active tools and additional motion, fan or heater telemetry when the printer provides it. Moonraker installations can expose information for up to twelve extruders.

Can I view my printer camera without using a cloud service?

Yes, when the printer and its local camera source are supported. PrintDeck connects directly to the camera on your local network while the camera view is open. No PrintDeck cloud account or remote camera proxy is required.

Will I need to flash KNOMI over USB after every update?

Normally, no. The initial PrintDeck installation uses the guided browser installer. Later supported releases can be installed over the air, with a configuration-preserving USB update available as a recovery option.
