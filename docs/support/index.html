Help us support more Bambu Lab models

After installing PrintDeck, open Web Config and choose My Printers → Add printer → Bambu Lab → Check compatibility. PrintDeck will create a report with private data removed, which helps us support more models. Download it and email it to support@printdeck.xyz. PrintDeck does not send the report automatically.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Can PrintDeck control my printer?

No. PrintDeck is made for monitoring. It works on your local Wi-Fi network, needs no cloud service and does not send printer data outside your network.

The only exception is the chamber light. PrintDeck can switch it on or off only when the compatibility check confirms that this is safe for your printer model. Everything happens locally on the device.

Why can’t I see my printer or some features?

Printer manufacturers do not use one common communication standard. PrintDeck handles many differences between brands and models, but it cannot recognize every variant.

Run Compatibility Check. PrintDeck gathers the required data locally, removes private information from the report and lets you download it. The report is not sent automatically—download it and email it to us.

This report helps us support your printer model.

Where can I find other enclosures?

We provide free models for three enclosures that you can print yourself. You are also welcome to create your own modifications and designs.

Need help building your own display? Contact us about compatible components, enclosure options, firmware installation and individual assembly assistance.

Display hardware is manufactured by third parties.
