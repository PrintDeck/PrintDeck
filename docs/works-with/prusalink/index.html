Before you begin

Connect in three steps

Enable PrusaLink for your printer and check that it opens from another device on the same network. Have its local address and the matching PrusaLink login or API key ready.

  1. 01

    Open PrusaLink locally

    Follow Prusa’s instructions for your printer model. Copy the local address and connection credentials from your printer or PrusaLink setup.

  2. 02

    Choose PrusaLink

    In Web Config, open My Printers → Add printer → Other printers. Enter the address and select PrusaLink under Connection type.

  3. 03

    Check your credentials

    Choose Username and password or API key to match your setup. Enter the details, then choose Check & add and select the online profile.

Good to know

Questions and answers

Do I need a Prusa Connect account?

This connection uses PrusaLink on your local network. Enter the local PrusaLink address and credentials, rather than your Prusa Connect account details.

Does every PrusaLink version provide the same data?

Available readings vary with the printer and PrusaLink version. PrintDeck displays the information supplied by the local connection.
