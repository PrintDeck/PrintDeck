SUPPORTED INPUTS

Start with an animation
or a still image.

Web Config converts every accepted source into a device-ready GIF with an opaque black background.

ANIMATED

GIF and animated WebP

Keep motion, select the useful moment and let PrintDeck sample the clip into a compact loop.

GIF87aGIF89aAnimated WebP
STILL

PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF and BMP

A still image becomes a single-frame GIF. HEIC and HEIF also work when your browser can decode them.

PNGJPEGWebPAVIFBMPHEIC / HEIF*

Video files are not accepted directly. Convert MP4, MOV or WebM to GIF or animated WebP first. Browser support determines whether HEIC and HEIF can be opened.

A SMALL EDITOR, RIGHT WHERE YOU NEED IT

Make it fit
before it uploads.

01

Click or drag and drop

Choose an image with the file picker, or drop it directly onto a reaction card in Web Config.

02

Zoom and reposition

Use the slider or mouse wheel for 1×–4× zoom, then drag the image to choose the square crop.

03

Choose the best moment

For longer animations, drag the timeline window and select a clip from 1 to 5 seconds. Shorter animations are used in full.

CLEAR LIMITS, AUTOMATIC OPTIMIZATION

Bring the source.
PrintDeck makes it fit.

The browser prepares the image for your exact display profile and reduces frames, colours or detail only when needed to stay within the current storage budget.

Supported formats: GIF and animated GIF, animated WebP, PNG, JPG / JPEG, WebP, AVIF, BMP and HEIC / HEIF when supported by your browser.
