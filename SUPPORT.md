# PrintDeck support

The [PrintDeck Support page](https://printdeck.xyz/support/)
is the starting point for the user manual, troubleshooting, project support,
DIY set enquiries and complete-device enquiries.

## Before requesting help

- Update to the latest stable PrintDeck release when possible.
- Check the [user manual](https://printdeck.xyz/printdeck-user-manual.pdf).
- Use the [Web Installer](https://printdeck.xyz/firmware/)
  for first installation, factory reset or a USB update.
- Check the [bill of materials](docs/BOM.md) when working with a supported
  enclosure.

## Reporting a firmware problem

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/PrintDeck/PrintDeck/issues) for a
reproducible PrintDeck firmware problem. Include:

- the PrintDeck firmware version;
- the round AMOLED or square LCD hardware variant;
- the printer brand, model and connection type;
- clear steps to reproduce the problem;
- what you expected and what happened instead;
- sanitized screenshots or logs when they help explain the problem.

Never publish Wi-Fi credentials, LAN access codes, API keys, tokens, printer
serial numbers, private addresses, SSIDs or private job names. Check every
screenshot and log before attaching it.

Security vulnerabilities must not be reported in a public issue. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Independently sourced hardware

The PrintDeck bill of materials describes the reference hardware. It is not a
shopping guide, compatibility list or wiring manual. Independently sourced
batteries, Qi receivers, speakers, cables and connectors must be verified by
the builder before connection. PrintDeck Support cannot confirm an untested
part from its listing, connector appearance or wire colours.

If you are not comfortable verifying polarity, voltage, insulation and power
routing, contact PrintDeck Support about a DIY set or complete device.

## DIY sets, complete devices and commercial licensing

Contact [PrintDeck Support](mailto:support@printdeck.xyz) about official DIY sets,
complete devices or a separate written license for commercial use of PrintDeck.
For commercial licensing, briefly describe the intended product, use and
distribution model.
